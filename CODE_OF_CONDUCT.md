# Code of Conduct

DataSum is intended to be useful for statisticians, professors, scientists, students, and applied analysts across many communities. Project discussions should stay respectful, specific, and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of different levels of statistical and programming experience.
- Critique ideas, methods, and code without personal attacks.
- Give enough context for others to understand your reasoning.
- Be open to correction when evidence or testing shows a better path.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Personal attacks or sustained unconstructive criticism.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Derailing technical or statistical discussions with unrelated arguments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or close comments and issues that violate this code of conduct. Repeated or severe violations may lead to blocking from the project.
